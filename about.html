About Jinan Bison Machinery

Jinan Bison Machinery Co., Ltd. is a professional commercial vehicle exporter based in China, specializing in providing reliable transportation solutions for customers worldwide, especially in Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and South America.

With years of experience in international trade and vehicle export, Bison Machinery focuses on supplying high-quality new trucks, used trucks, semi trailers, buses, and truck spare parts to meet the different needs of global transportation businesses.

We are committed to customized design, reliable quality, efficient logistics solutions and professional after-sales support. Our goal is to help customers save time and cost while building long-term partnerships based on trust and performance.
